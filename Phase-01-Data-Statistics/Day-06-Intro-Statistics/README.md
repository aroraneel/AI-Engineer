# Day 6 — What is Statistics, Types of Statistics, Population vs Sample

**Topics:** what is statistics; types of statistics; population vs sample

## What I Learned

- Statistics is the science of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data to draw conclusions and make decisions under uncertainty. Every ML model is, underneath, statistics operating at scale.
- **Descriptive statistics** summarizes data you already have (mean, median, mode, std dev, charts) — no generalizing beyond it.
- **Inferential statistics** uses a smaller sample to make generalizations or predictions about a larger group you haven't fully measured (hypothesis testing, confidence intervals, polling).
- **Population** = the entire group being studied. **Sample** = a smaller, actually-measured subset of that population.
- ML training data is almost always a sample, not the full population of "all possible data" for a problem — a core part of ML is ensuring that sample is representative enough for the resulting model to generalize to new, unseen data.
